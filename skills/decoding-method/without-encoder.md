# Decoding Without an Encoder

Rules for decoding a target with no encoder available: it is known only through real-world
samples, with no encoder source to read and no way to encode new input. The general rules in
[rules.md](rules.md) apply throughout.

No rules yet. A rule enters only when work on a target without an encoder has proved it, with its
incident recorded in [incidents.md](incidents.md).
